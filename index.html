Frequently Asked Questions

Can I renew my passport by mail?

Often yes, if your most recent passport is undamaged, was issued when you were 16 or older and meets other conditions. We help you check whether you qualify before you apply.


What if my passport expires soon?

Many countries require at least six months of validity beyond your travel dates. We recommend renewing early and can help you plan around your trip.


Are you a government agency?

No. We are a private passport assistance service and are not affiliated with the U.S. Department of State. Government fees are separate from our service fees.
